## PRICING DECIDED — owner confirmed 2026-11-06

Pricing model: **fixed-price offline assessment** (two weeks, no integration,
deliverable = signed evidence pack). Three bands, per market:

| Market | Fixed price per assessment | Why this band |
| ------ | -------------------------- | ------------- |
| Egypt (local BPO/CX) | EGP 60,000–90,000 (~$1,200–1,800) | 10–20× a junior consultant day-rate; still below a Big-4 vendor-assessment engagement |
| Gulf (UAE/KSA/Qatar BPO) | $2,500–4,000 | Gulf compliance budgets are USD-denominated; one questionnaire response cycle saved pays for it |
| Global (EU/UK BPO serving banks/telcos) | $3,000–6,000 | EU AI Act questionnaire pressure is highest here; price against the cost of one failed vendor review |

Terms: 50% upfront, 50% on pack delivery. Annual re-run (refreshed pack,
same mapping) at 40% of the assessment price. First pilot was free —
use it as the reference case, do not discount below the band afterwards.
